02 INDUSTRY 4.0

AI in Manufacturing

Sub-millimeter defect detection & inline quality inspection at conveyor speed.

Engineered for demanding industrial production lines. Our human-in-the-loop systems inspect casting, machining, metalwork, and precision assembly parts — flagging cracks, porosity, burrs, and dimensional defects without halting production lines.

✓Automated visual defect classification at line speed
✓Human-in-the-loop validation & continuous learning
✓Turnkey PLC & robotic reject actuator integration

03 CLINICAL INTELLIGENCE

AI in Healthcare

Assistive diagnostic imaging, specimen screening & clinical intelligence.

Bringing state-of-the-art computer vision to diagnostic workflows. Our privacy-first, on-premise neural networks assist clinicians in identifying subtle anomalies across X-rays, CT scans, and histological samples with high sensitivity and operator verification.

✓Radiology & histological anomaly decision-support
✓100% on-premise, HIPAA & GDPR privacy compliance
✓Assistive clinician dashboards with confidence metrics

04 INTELLIGENT SECURITY

AI-based Surveillance System

Perimeter protection, real-time intrusion tracking & industrial safety.

Transforming passive cameras into active, sub-second threat sentinels. Our edge models track unauthorized perimeter incursions, recognize safety gear non-compliance (helmets, vests), and notify command teams with near-zero false alarms.

✓Real-time multi-camera spatial tracking & alerts
✓Industrial PPE & restricted hazard-zone monitoring
✓Sub-second edge compute with sub-5% false alarm rate
